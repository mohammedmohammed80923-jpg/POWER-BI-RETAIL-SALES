# POWER-BI-RETAIL-SALES
Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics Dashboard
An interactive and comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to transform raw retail data into actionable insights, tracking profitability, product performance, cost structures, and customer payment behaviors.

🚀 Project Overview
This project provides a 360-degree view of retail operations. It helps stakeholders analyze top-performing product categories, monitor core financial metrics (costs, prices, and profits), and evaluate the impact of discount rates across different payment methods.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights critical financial metrics at a glance:

Total Cost: 25.06M

Total Profit (INR): 6.35M

Unit Price (Avg): 8.48M

Unit Cost (Avg): 6.18M

📊 Dashboard Components & Features
1. Product Profitability Breakdown
Donut Chart Analysis: Visualizes the share of profits contributed by each product category.

Top Performer: Laptops dominate the profit pool, accounting for 50.4% (3.20M) of the total profits, followed by Mobile Phones and Tablets.

2. Multi-Metric Product Performance
Combo Charts: Tracks the relationship between Sum of Profit (INR), Gross Sales (INR), and Quantity sold across products (Laptop, Mobile Phone, Tablet, Monitor, Printer, Headphones, Keyboard, Mouse).

3. Payment Method & Discount Analysis
Waterfall Chart: Breaks down the Sum of Discount Rate across various payment channels (Card, Net Banking, Cash, and UPI), helping identify which transaction methods incur the highest discounts.

4. Goal & Target Tracking
Gauge Visuals: Measures current performance metrics (such as a total profit of 6.35M) against target thresholds (up to 12.69M) to track goal completion.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
Data Transformation & Modeling: Power Query (ETL, Data Cleaning)

Calculations & Measures: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated columns and custom metrics.

Data Visualization: Power BI Desktop (Donut Charts, Waterfall Charts, KPI Cards, Gauge Charts, Line & Column Combos).

💡 Key Business Insights
Product Concentration: A significant portion of the total revenue and profit is heavily concentrated in high-value electronics, specifically Laptops.

Discount Optimization: Payment method tracking shows varying discount trends that can be optimized to protect profit margins.

Cost vs. Margin Visibility: Clear segregation of unit prices and unit costs enables rapid evaluation of product profitability.
